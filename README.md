# Erstwählerforum 2026 Homepage
Cross-school cooperation project for civic education for young people, especially first-time voters.

# Website Structure & Content Briefing: Erstwählerforum 2026

**Purpose of this document:** Blueprint for the Erstwählerforum 2026 website. This briefing serves as a template for agents and the team. It takes the existing design into account (dark mode, card layout) and protects the conceptual IP.

## Sitemap Overview
*   `/` (homepage) – hub & quick overview
*   `/konzept` – overview of the event formats
*   `/zeitplan` – the daily schedule for students
*   `/team` – team overview & `/team/member` (detailed profiles)
*   `/schulen` – introduction of the participating partner schools
*   `/blog` – latest news & updates (already exists)
*   `/presse` – press releases & press contact
*   `/kontakt` – FAQ & inquiries
*   `/impressum` – legal notice (already exists)

---

## 1. Homepage (`/`)
**Goal:** A short, concise entry point. Visual focus on the date and purpose.

*   **Hero section:** Headline "Erstwählerforum 2026", date (June 2), location (Stadeum).
*   **Quick links:** 3-4 teaser cards (layout similar to your blog design) for:
    *   **Program:** "What happens on June 2?" -> `/zeitplan`
    *   **The idea:** "Our approach to civic education." -> `/konzept`
    *   **News:** teaser for the latest blog post -> `/blog`
*   **CTA:** "Submit a question for the discussions" (link to Slido).

## 2. The Concept (`/konzept`)
**IP protection:** We describe what happens, but not in detail how we solve it organizationally.

*   **Moderated panels:** Discussions on topics relevant to young people, led by student teams. Focus on interaction instead of one-way presentation.
*   **Political marketplace:** Direct exchange at exhibition booths in the foyer and on the gallery.
*   **Impulse formats:** Short introductions by the parties as an entry point (speed-dating principle).

## 3. Daily Schedule (`/zeitplan`)
**Goal:** Orientation for the mandatory event.

*   Tabular overview (08:00 to 13:00).
*   **Roughly divided into:** admission, welcome/introduction, cycles (marketplace & discussions), joint closing session.
*   As in the document, the table should be divided into cycles 1, 2, and 3. However, on hover, two additional rows should effectively be inserted into the table for each cycle row, showing 45 minutes of discussion and 15 minutes of transition time.
